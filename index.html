Needle, Thread, Silence.

A tiny original tribute to pin-sharp Metroidvania platforming: hand-tuned run & jump physics, wall-clinging, a silk dash with real i-frames, and pogo-bouncing off silk pods — all wrapped in a hand-painted, lantern-lit insect hollow.

HOW TO MOVE
MOVEMENT

Coyote time & buffered jumps

Every jump is forgiving by design: a grace window after leaving a ledge, and an early press is remembered until you land.

SILK DASH

Dash with real i-frames

A short, committed burst with a handful of invulnerable frames — enough to slip under a swing or skip a gap.

WALLS

Cling & wall-jump

Press into a wall to cling and slide slowly, then kick off at an angle to chain height out of narrow shafts.

NEEDLE

Pogo off silk pods

A downward strike on a glowing pod bounces you skyward and instantly refills your jump and dash.

The Mossbound Hollow — a movement playground

CHAMBER

Run the gauntlet

One long vertical slice built to exercise every tool: ledges, a dash-gap, a wall-jump chimney and a pogo run over spikes.

BENCHES

Rest & recover

Touch a bench to fully heal and set your respawn point, just like a quiet moment between trials.

FOLIAGE

Breakable clutter

Slash grass, pots and silk pods for a satisfying little burst — purely for the joy of it.
