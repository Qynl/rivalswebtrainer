1V1 ARENA SHOOTER · BEST OF 5

Slide. Jump. Slice. Repeat.

Fast, fluid movement with slides, slide-jumps and a scythe dash. Pick your guns, then take on a bot in five rounds. Runs in your browser, no install.

HOW TO PLAY
MOVEMENT

Slide and slide-jump

Slide to keep speed, then jump out of it for a launch. Hold jump to auto-hop and carry speed.

WEAPONS

Guns with character

Assault rifle, run-and-gun SMG, marksman rifle and a sidearm. Each has its own range and recoil.

SCYTHE

Slash and dash

Close range is the scythe's domain. Its dash closes gaps and dodges incoming fire.

BOTS

Four difficulty tiers

From Recruit to Nightmare. Each round is 90 seconds, first to three wins takes the match.
