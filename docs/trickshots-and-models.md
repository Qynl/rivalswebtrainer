# Trickshot, Bow and shared-model pass

## Combat rules

Self-health damage is rejected at the offline `Game.damage` boundary and the authoritative `Match.damage` boundary. Explosion processing deliberately does **not** skip the owner's entire entity: the original RPG/launcher/grenade launch impulse remains. Own Flashbang blindness is skipped. Molotov ticks skip their owner while still damaging opponents. Teammate rules and enemy hits remain unchanged.

Knife F starts a 105 ms pending impact with a separate 0.55-second recovery; an RPG cooldown is not a blocker. It retains the selected gun slot/ammunition, cancels reload/heal/burst/inspect, and displays a temporary Knife model if needed. The initiating slot and currently equipped melee are revalidated at impact, as are life, range, cone and LOS. It uses the existing facing-based 40/150 Knife rule, not a teleport or an unconditional backstab. Death/switching cancels a pending hit; repeated input cannot shorten recovery. Server inputs pass through the same implementation. Normal Knife LMB remains a normal melee attack. Fists F does not invent an attack.

## Presentation

- **Bow:** new compound geometry, carbon limbs, independent pivots/cams, sight and pulling hand. Shared `BOW_DRAW_TIME` is 0.8 seconds across shot readiness, animation and HUD. Limb/cam endpoints bend together while the nock, arrow and pulling hand retreat. Release adds a short string vibration; empty/reloading states hide the nocked arrow. Arrows are slim, tipped and fletched, follow their trajectory, and use the existing bounded trace pool. Launchers orient their detailed ballistic shells along the velocity; rockets have fins, an ogive and a small pulsing exhaust. All are created by the same offline/online projectile factory.
- **Weapons:** shared finish palette and procedural family-specific receiver panels, rails, vents, screws, controls, foregrips, barrels, magazines, scopes, launcher/Minigun/Flamethrower hardware and melee grip/blade details. Armored gloves remain attached to moving weapon parts. Rifle is 20 meshes with hands; moving subassemblies are separately batched. Existing sight anchors and mechanical animations are retained.
- **Feel:** per-weapon short snap/recovery profiles and faster ADS. Kick changes only local weapon transforms, not camera yaw/pitch or bullet aim. Roll direction uses a dedicated local shot sequence that is not reset by authoritative statistics reconciliation, not derived from alternating render-frame timestamps. Knife F has its own compact thrust/recovery. Hit marker scale pulses restart on accepted confirmations without allowing lower-priority body hits to erase a headshot/kill; the initial opacity is immediate. Floating damage numbers and headshot sounds have stronger transient emphasis.
- **Characters:** shared original Striker model replaces the old robot-like armor. Human facial features, hair, goggles, headsets, ceramic plates, woven bump texture, equipment/pouches and jointed boots/knees improve silhouette/material definition. The skeleton, shoulder/elbow/hand IK targets, crouch/slide/death anchors and physical capsules do not change. Offline and online actors use actual detailed gun models. Held-weapon assemblies are excluded from character rigid-bone batching so their magazine/bolt/flash parts stay functional.
- **Resources:** no external textures/models or runtime downloads. Small character fabric textures are locally generated. Materials/geometry are batched; transient trails reuse the existing 64-slot pool. Render updates do not allocate new geometry. Visual disposal handles texture maps and material arrays, including replaced character equipment.

## Validation

- `npm test`: 265 passing tests. New focused coverage includes owner immunity vs enemy damage, explosive launch velocity, own fire/flash, delayed front/rear Knife F, spam/reload/slot/cover/range behavior, Bow linkage and reload visibility, per-weapon finish/geometry stability, and character anchors/mesh bounds. An authoritative test executes RPG/launcher → F backstab while preserving owner HP and gun selection and checking emitted impulse/damage events.
- `tests/trickshots.spec.js`: two real Chromium/WebGL checks execute the launch/stab combo without changing aim, then render the new shared character rig and all 23 weapons. Bow full-draw and character front screenshots are written to `/tmp` for inspection, not committed as generated assets.
- Production build includes the updated models, feedback and offline cache. The full 27-test Chromium suite passed, including multiplayer lobbies/authority, all map collision layouts, rendering/contact shading, AI combat, controls and disconnected production reload. Final Bow sight/hand clearance and local shot-sequence refinements were rechecked in a **7/7 passing** focused browser run, including two-client authoritative multiplayer and all 23 weapon renders. The final production build also passed (2.93 seconds).

Browser checks use software WebGL in this sandbox; they establish rendered correctness and behavior, not a representative user-device frame rate. The live development server currently runs authoritative WebSocket fallback because native geckos transport is unavailable in this environment; this pass does not claim UDP validation.
